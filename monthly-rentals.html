For tenants

How renting with IDC works

💬

1. Enquire

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates. We'll answer questions, send more photos and arrange a viewing or video walk-through.

🪪

2. Apply

We ask for ID, visa details and a little about your stay. It keeps things safe for you, the owner and the neighbours.

📝

3. Sign

A clear monthly agreement, one month's deposit and the first month's rent. What's included, and what isn't, is written down.

🔑

4. Move in

We walk the villa with you and record its condition. Our team cleans and changes the linen every week, and stays on call for maintenance for as long as you're there.

For owners
