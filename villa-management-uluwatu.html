Questions

Villa management in Uluwatu: FAQ

How much does villa management cost in Uluwatu?

We charge a flat 20% of gross revenue, with no setup costs. Cleaning supplies, maintenance and other third-party costs are passed through at cost and shown in full in your monthly report and live dashboard.

Which booking sites will my villa be listed on?

Airbnb, Booking.com and VRBO as standard, plus our own direct booking site, balivillabookings.com. One synced calendar keeps them from double-booking.

How do you handle Uluwatu's quieter months?

With seasonal pricing, shorter minimum stays and, where it suits the villa, monthly rentals to fill gaps. Our seasons guide explains the calendar.

Can I still stay in my villa?

Of course. Give us reasonable notice and we block out the dates and plan bookings around your stays.

I'm buying in Uluwatu. Can you help?

Yes. Water supply, road access and permits vary from villa to villa on the Bukit. Our buyer's service checks zoning, title, lease, permits, the site and real income, and includes a free zoning check.
