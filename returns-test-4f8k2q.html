Free Tool

Can this villa legally be rented out?

Many Bali villas sit on land where villas aren't allowed, and buyers find out too late. Paste any villa's location and see its official zoning in seconds, straight from Badung's government plan.

Type the villa's name as it shows in Google Maps, paste its Share link, or long-press the roof in Google Maps and copy the numbers. Covers Badung: Canggu, Seminyak, Kuta, Jimbaran, Uluwatu and Nusa Dua.

Double-check it officially

Our tool is a fast first check. Before you pay a deposit, confirm zoning with the government's own systems:

  • GISTARU, the land ministry's official spatial-plan map, covering Badung, Gianyar (Ubud) and Denpasar (Sanur): gistaru.atrbpn.go.id
  • BHUMI, to confirm the plot on the land certificate is the one you're looking at: bhumi.atrbpn.go.id
  • KKPR, the government's binding confirmation that a use is allowed at that spot, issued through the OSS licensing system. Your notary or licensing agent can check or obtain it.

Our Villa Check covers all three for you.

If sold, income and costs rise each year at the growth rates shown (the table's income rows show the first year), the Bali sale price is reduced by part of the lease used up (the box above sets how much; land value in good areas can hold up the price), and selling costs come off both sales. With tax on, capital gains tax is charged on the profit from each sale at the rate shown (Australia: the 50% discount is replaced from July 2027 by indexing the purchase price for inflation, then taxing the gain at your normal rate; the rules are new and still settling, so check them), and Indonesian tax on a Bali sale is not included. Price growth is your own guess, not a forecast, and can be negative. Bali prices can rise in popular areas, but a lease loses value as the years left shrink, so check the length of the lease; capital gains tax is not included on either side. Home income is rent less running costs and loan interest. With tax on, a loss at home is not used to reduce other income, and Bali income is taxed at the Indonesian rate shown only. Your home country may also tax overseas income, usually with a credit for the Indonesian tax, so check your own position. Tax rules differ by person and change, so check yours with an adviser.
