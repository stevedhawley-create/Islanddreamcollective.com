Ubud draws a different guest to the coast: people here for rice-field and jungle views, wellness, culture and quiet. Many stay longer, and they choose villas on the view, the pool and the feeling of the place.

The climate is greener and wetter, so villas need more care, especially through the wet season. Island Dream Collective manages villas for owners who live overseas and keeps them guest-ready year round, with every booking and expense in your own live dashboard.

Questions

Villa management in Ubud: FAQ

How much does villa management cost in Ubud?

We charge a flat 20% of gross revenue, with no setup costs. Cleaning supplies, maintenance and other third-party costs are passed through at cost and shown in full in your monthly report and live dashboard.

Do you manage monthly rentals in Ubud?

Yes. Monthly management starts from 15% of rent collected. See monthly rentals.

How do you handle the wet season?

With regular inspections, airing and mould prevention, and repairs booked before the rains where possible. Our wet season checklist shows what we look at.

Can I still stay in my villa?

Of course. Give us reasonable notice and we block out the dates and plan bookings around your stays.

I'm buying in Ubud. Can you help?

Yes. Our buyer's service checks zoning, title, lease, permits, the site and real income before you commit.
