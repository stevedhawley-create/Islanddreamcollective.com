How It Works

From first chat
to keys and income.

You stay in Australia; our Bali team does the legwork and you see everything as it happens.

01

Your brief

Budget, areas, bedrooms, freehold or leasehold, and what you want it to earn. We agree the brief on a call.

02

We find and check

We shortlist established villas from agencies, private sellers and off-market contacts, then check each one: zoning, title, lease, permits, a site visit with video, and the real booking income.

03

Buy, then earn

We negotiate, coordinate the notary and settlement, then Island Dream Collective can manage the villa from day one, with your own owner dashboard.

What We Check

Seven checks
before you commit.

✓

It's really for sale

Confirmed with the agent or owner, at the real price.

✓

Zoning

Official zone and whether villas and rentals are allowed there.

✓

Land certificate

Owner, loans and disputes, checked at the land office through a licensed notary.

✓

Lease

Years left, extension terms, and that the lessor really owns the land.

✓

Permits

Building permit (PBG/IMB), SLF and rental licence match the villa.

✓

Site visit

Road access, water, power, flooding and build quality, with photos and video.

✓

Real income

Booking statements, not projections, compared with villas we manage nearby.
